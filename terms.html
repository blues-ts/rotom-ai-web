2. The Service

River AI TCG helps Pokémon TCG collectors identify cards, look up prices, track what they own and what they're selling, and chat with an AI assistant ("River") about the market. Features include:

  • Search and browse across cards, sets, and sealed products (English and Japanese)
  • Live pricing and recent sold-listing data from major marketplaces
  • Price-history charts and recent comparable sales
  • On-device card scanning with the camera — one card at a time, or a full binder page
  • Collections, with the exact variant, condition, or grade of each copy and an optional price paid
  • Favorites and watchlists
  • A selling inventory ("Vending") for tracking asking prices and completed sales
  • Portfolio value tracking, with a daily history and cost-basis comparison
  • AI-driven market analysis — momentum, volatility, grade-spread, and liquidity "signals"
  • A conversational AI assistant ("River") that can answer questions about your own collection and prepare actions for you to confirm
  • Premium features under the "River AI TCG Pro" subscription

The Service is currently offered on iOS. It requires an active internet connection — your collection is stored on our servers, and the app is not designed to work offline.

Card scanning is available on iOS.
