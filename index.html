Common questions

Still have a question? Write to the team. One of us will read it.

What is River AI TCG?×

River is an iPhone app that identifies trading cards, shows current raw and graded values, and tracks your collection as the market changes.

Which cards does River support?＋

More than 50,000 English and Japanese cards, along with popular sealed products.

Does scanning upload my photos?＋

No. Recognition runs on your device. Camera frames and scan photos are not sent to River or any third party.

Which grading companies are included?＋

PSA, BGS, CGC, ACE, TAG, AGS, and SGC pricing is supported.

Where does the pricing come from?＋

River uses aggregated marketplace pricing, recent comparable sales, and listings, and shows the sources inside the app.

What happens if my Pro subscription ends?＋

Your data is retained. Resubscribing restores your collections, favorites, inventory, and portfolio history.
