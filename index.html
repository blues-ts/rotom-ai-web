About the app

What River AI TCG does.

Scan Live prices Graded comps Collection value AI answers

River AI TCG is a mobile app for people who collect, trade, and sell trading cards. Point your phone's camera at a card and the app identifies the exact card, set, and variant, then shows what it is currently selling for — as a raw card or graded by PSA, BGS, CGC, ACE, TAG, AGS, or SGC — using prices gathered continuously from card marketplaces.

Save cards to a collection and the app values your whole binder in real time, with price history from the last 7 days out to all time. You can scan a stack of cards in one session, look up sealed products like booster boxes and elite trainer boxes, browse every set in English and Japanese, and organize inventory for selling at a show. River, the built-in AI assistant, answers questions about any card in plain language and shows the market data and recent sales it based its answer on.

Who it's for: collectors valuing what they own, traders checking a deal at the table, and vendors pricing inventory before a show. Free to try — sign in with Apple or Google to search every card, set, and sealed product. River AI TCG Pro unlocks live prices, the AI assistant, and collections synced across your devices.
