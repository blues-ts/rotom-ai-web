Common questions

Still have a question? Write to the team. One of us will read it.

What is River TCG?×

River is an iPhone app for Pokémon card collectors. Scan a card or a full binder page, see current raw and graded values, track your collections as the market moves, and ask River AI before you buy, sell, or grade.

What’s free and what needs River Pro?＋

With a free account you can search every set, see Near Mint raw prices, and keep one collection of up to 25 cards. River Pro unlocks scanning, every condition and graded price, price history, unlimited collections, sealed products, favorites, vendor mode, River Lens, and River AI.

How much is River Pro?＋

$3.99 per week, $9.99 per month, or $79.99 per year in the US. Every option includes the same features. Subscriptions auto-renew until you cancel in your App Store settings.

Which cards does River support?＋

More than 50,000 English and Japanese Pokémon cards, plus popular sealed products.

Does scanning upload my photos?＋

No. Recognition runs on your iPhone. Camera frames and scan photos are not sent to River or any third party.

What is River Lens?＋

River Lens shows card prices while you watch streams or shop in other apps. Start a screen broadcast from River, and prices for cards on your screen appear in the Dynamic Island and on your Lock Screen. Recognition happens on your iPhone, and only card IDs are sent to look up prices. River Lens currently matches English cards.

Which grading companies are included?＋

PSA, CGC, BGS, SGC, ACE, TAG, and AGS, with prices for each grade.

Where does the pricing come from?＋

River uses aggregated marketplace pricing, recent comparable sales, and listings. Each card links out to TCGplayer and eBay sold listings so you can check the comps yourself.

What happens if my Pro subscription ends?＋

Your data is kept. Resubscribing restores your collections, favorites, inventory, and portfolio history.
